verdetto: "This isn't an ideological choice, it depends on how far your process diverges from the standard. If the flow is common in your sector and you want results now with low risk, a ready vertical SaaS is often the rational pick: you pay little, go live in days, maintenance isn't your problem. But if that process is a competitive advantage, the data is sensitive, or no SaaS covers your exceptions, the custom agent pays back: more upfront, but it kills the perpetual fee and leaves you code and control. Many SMEs do well to start with SaaS where the process is standard and build custom only where the process is genuinely their own."
