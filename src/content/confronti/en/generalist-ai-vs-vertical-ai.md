verdetto: "It is not a binary choice: the two coexist. Generalist AI is the cheapest, fastest way to get the team AI-literate and cover a thousand micro-tasks, but it stays generic and does not run your processes. Vertical AI costs more upfront, yet on a core, repetitive, high-volume process it pays back with measurable ROI and precision. The right question is not 'which AI', but 'which processes deserve a dedicated agent and where a generic tool is enough'. For most B2B SMEs: generalist for everyone, vertical on the 2-3 processes that weigh most."
