inBreve: "For most B2B SMEs, use generalist AI (horizontal, e.g. ChatGPT or Copilot) for broad productivity and early experiments, and move to vertical AI (industry-specific, a custom-built agent) when a core, repetitive process absorbs so many hours, often across several people, that their cost exceeds the agent's. Generalist AI is cheap and instant but stays generic; vertical AI costs more upfront but executes the process with precision on your data. It is not either-or: they almost always coexist."

optionA:
  nome: "Generalist AI (horizontal)"
  descrizione: "General purpose tools like ChatGPT, Claude or Microsoft Copilot, used by people to write, summarise, search and reason on any topic."
  pro:
    - "Low, immediate cost: business licences of about $20-30 per person per month"
    - "Zero setup: people start today, no project needed"
    - "Covers a thousand different use cases, from email to brainstorming"
    - "Great to get the team AI-literate before investing more"
  contro:
    - "It does not know your processes, data and rules: generic answers"
    - "Output depends heavily on who writes the prompt"
    - "The new agents run workflows on connected apps, but not on systems without an integration, such as many ERPs"
    - "Data and compliance risk if used without governance"
  idealePer:
    - "Broad individual productivity across the whole company"
    - "SMEs early in their AI journey wanting low-risk experiments"
    - "Creative and research work that is not standardised"
optionB:
  nome: "Vertical AI (industry-specific)"
  descrizione: "AI agents built for a specific process (CV screening, reconciliations, lead qualification), integrated with your data and systems."
  pro:
    - "Executes the process end-to-end, not just suggests"
    - "High accuracy because it works on your data and rules"
    - "Measurable ROI on a process (e.g. hours saved per week)"
    - "Built-in governance and audit trail, useful for GDPR and the AI Act"
  contro:
    - "Higher upfront cost"
    - "Only makes sense on repetitive, high-volume processes"
    - "Needs an internal owner to follow the project"
    - "Must be maintained when the process or systems change"
    - "On processes such as CV screening it is high-risk under the AI Act: from 2 December 2027 you need human oversight and logs"
  idealePer:
    - "Core, repetitive, high-volume processes"
    - "B2B SMEs wanting measurable ROI, not just vague productivity"
    - "Companies with compliance and decision-traceability needs"

verdetto: "It is not a binary choice: the two coexist. Generalist AI is the cheapest, fastest way to get the team AI-literate and cover a thousand micro-tasks, but it stays generic and, even with the new agents, works only on the apps you connect. Vertical AI costs more upfront, yet on a core, repetitive, high-volume process it pays back with measurable ROI and precision. The right question is not 'which AI', but 'which processes deserve a dedicated agent and where a generic tool is enough'. For most B2B SMEs: generalist for everyone, vertical on the 2-3 processes that weigh most."
faq:
  - q: "If I already use ChatGPT in the company, do I still need a vertical agent?"
    a: "It depends on the process. ChatGPT is great for individual productivity and, with workspace agents in preview on business plans, it can run recurring workflows on connected apps (<a href='https://openai.com/business/workspace-agents/'>OpenAI</a>). But it does not know your rules and does not reach systems without an integration. When a process is repetitive, high-volume and core (CV screening, reconciliations, lead qualification), a <a href='/ai-agents'>vertical agent</a> delivers measurable ROI a generic tool cannot. The two integrate, they do not exclude each other."
  - q: "How much does it cost to move from a generalist tool to a vertical agent?"
    a: "A generalist tool costs a licence per person: ChatGPT Business $20 a month billed annually or $25 monthly (<a href='https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq'>OpenAI</a>), Claude Team $20 or $25 (<a href='https://claude.com/pricing'>Anthropic</a>), Microsoft 365 Copilot $30 paid yearly (<a href='https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/copilot-studio'>Microsoft</a>). With Soraia a vertical agent starts from <strong>€8,000/month</strong>: three dedicated people (an Account Manager, a Project Manager and an AI Engineer) and one release in production every month, with first delivery in 4 weeks. The jump makes sense when the time lost on a single process justifies the investment. To see which processes are worth it, <a href='/parliamone'>let's talk</a>."
  - q: "Does vertical AI replace generalist AI?"
    a: "No. Generalist AI stays the everyday tool for a thousand non-standardised tasks. Vertical AI steps in on core, repetitive processes that need precision, execution and traceability. In practice an SME uses both: <a href='/guide/agenti-ai-aziende'>here is how vertical agents work</a>."
  - q: "How do I know which processes deserve a vertical agent?"
    a: "Look for processes with three traits: repetitive, high volume, and eating hours from skilled people. On these, a vertical agent brings clear ROI, if the hours saved each month are worth more than its cost. Watch out for processes about people: CV screening, for example, is high-risk under the AI Act and from 2 December 2027 requires human oversight and logs (<a href='https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj'>AI Act</a>, <a href='https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj'>Digital Omnibus</a>). For everything else, a generalist tool is more than enough."
