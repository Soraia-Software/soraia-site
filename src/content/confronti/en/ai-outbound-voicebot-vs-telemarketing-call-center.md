verdetto: "It is not black or white, and often not even an either/or. The AI voicebot wins on high, repetitive volumes: qualifying contacts who gave consent, callbacks, appointment reminders, where it slashes cost per call and frees reps for what matters. The human call center stays superior on complex negotiations and high-value sales, where empathy and objection handling make the difference. In Italy, though, a promotional voicebot may only call people who consented to automated calls, while a call center with human operators works on lists not in the do-not-call register; and the voicebot must disclose it is an AI under the AI Act. The pattern that works for most SMEs is hybrid: AI filters and warms up leads, humans close."
