verdetto: "The right question is not 'WhatsApp or website', but 'where are my customers when they need me'. If your audience already messages you on WhatsApp and you live on follow-ups and asynchronous chats, start there, accepting Meta's fees and Meta's policy walls (approved templates, possible rule changes). If you have web traffic to convert and want full control over data and UX, the website chatbot pays off more and with no per-conversation cost. In practice the most robust choice for an SME is to build a single AI agent on the process, then expose it on the channel (or channels) where customers actually are."
