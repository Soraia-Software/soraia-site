verdetto: "This isn't a prestige contest, it's a question of fit. For an SME that wants to automate a process and see a measurable result, a specialized agency offers shorter timelines, predictable pricing and someone who answers by name: the code stays yours and the upfront risk is low. A Big4 makes sense when the project is genuinely multi-country, touches group governance, or needs a brand to get the decision through a board. The right question isn't 'who is bigger', but 'does my project truly need that scale?'."
