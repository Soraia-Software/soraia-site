  pro:
    - "Covers the phone channel, still dominant for many customers"

    - "No barrier for people who dislike chat or typing"
    - "Available 24/7 on a channel that usually goes unanswered after hours"

  nome: "AI text chatbot"
  descrizione: "An AI agent that replies in writing on the website, WhatsApp, email or messaging, handling FAQs, triage and handoff to the team."

    - "Cheaper and faster to launch: no voice complexity"
    - "Easy to test, fix and improve by reading conversations"
    - "A written record of every exchange, useful for audit and compliance"
    - "Integrates well with web, WhatsApp and email where customers already write"
  contro:
    - "Does not reach people who only want to call or dislike typing"
    - "On long or emotional requests text can feel cold"
    - "Still needs a good handoff to the team when the request gets complex"
    - "If poorly designed it becomes a maze of generic answers"
  idealePer:
    - "SMEs with younger customers or those used to chat and messaging"
    - "Teams that want to start early and at contained cost"
    - "Support with many FAQs and triage before the operator"

verdetto: "This is not an absolute contest between voice and text: it depends on where your customers are and what they ask. If most contact goes through the phone and repetitive calls clog the switchboard, a voicebot recovers real time, provided you nail voice quality and handoff. If customers already write on web or WhatsApp and requests are mostly FAQs and triage, a text chatbot costs less, launches faster and is easier to improve. For many SMEs the pragmatic move is to start with text, measure, and add voice only on the phone channel where volumes justify it."
