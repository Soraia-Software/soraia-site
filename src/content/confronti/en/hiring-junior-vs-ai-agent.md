verdetto: "It is not a real either/or: it depends on what the role actually does. If the load is high-volume, repetitive and rule-based (CV screening, invoice extraction, ticket triage), an AI agent covers it faster, at scale and at a lower marginal cost, while a junior would get bored and make mistakes. But if the role needs judgement, relationships with candidates or clients, or is the first rung of a career, a junior is irreplaceable. The most common move in SMEs is to combine them: the agent takes the mechanical part, the junior focuses on what needs a human head and grows faster."
